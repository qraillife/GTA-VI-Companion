QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

Does the countdown change when I select another timezone?

Yes. Rockstar announced release at 12:00 AM on 19 November in each region's local time. Selecting another timezone calculates midnight in that region, rather than simply translating one global release moment.

When does GTA VI release in Brisbane?

12:00 AM Australian Eastern Standard Time (AEST) on Thursday 19 November 2026, according to Rockstar's regional-midnight policy.

Can I pre-load GTA VI?

Yes. Rockstar confirmed pre-loading for eligible orders from 10 November 2026. Check your PlayStation or Xbox library.

Is GTA VI coming to PC, PS4 or Xbox One at launch?

The announced launch platforms are PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. No PC, PS4 or Xbox One launch date has been announced here.

Will GTA VI be 60 FPS?

Rockstar says GTA VI is targeting 30 frames per second on current-generation consoles. Check official announcements if a new performance option is announced.

Does this app provide live news automatically?

The app links to Rockstar's official Newswire for live updates. Headlines displayed within the app are a curated snapshot, rather than an automatically synced news feed.

Is this an official Rockstar Games app?

No. VI Companion is an independent fan-made information app. It does not host game downloads, sell keys or represent Rockstar Games.
